* ADR-0099: Bounded sparse one-shot schedule due paging
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: adr-0099-bounded-sparse-one-shot-schedule-due-paging
:END:
- *Status:* accepted
- *Date:* 2026-08-08
- *Decision and execution issue:* [[https://github.com/Knosence9/project-vela/issues/1001][#1001]]
- *Related:* ADR-0034, ADR-0093, ADR-0095

** Context
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: context
:END:
=ScheduleStore::list_due= provides complete fail-closed due discovery in due-instant then exact-ID order, but its storage work, replay, and allocation grow with the complete authoritative inventory. Ordinary exact-ID inventory pages bound work but leave due filtering and sparse continuation semantics to every caller.

A dense page of due matches could inspect an unbounded number of future or non-pending schedules. Ordering a durable keyset directly by the arbitrary =u64= due instant currently stored in JSON event payloads would also require either trusting malformed payloads during selection, using SQLite numeric representations that cannot preserve every =u64= exactly, or adding indexed schema and migration authority.

** Decision
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: decision
:END:
Add =ScheduleDuePage=, containing complete matching schedules and an optional =next_after= exact schedule ID. Add =ScheduleStore::list_due_page(cutoff, after, scan_size)=, reusing validated =SchedulePageSize= values from 1 through 1024 as the explicit bound on authoritative schedules inspected.

The event log selects at most =scan_size + 1= authoritative =schedule.created= streams after the optional caller-owned exclusive exact-ID cursor. It fully replays that exact-ID-ordered window in one read snapshot. Canonical projection validates every selected history, including lookahead, before filtering the first at most =scan_size= projections to schedules whose persisted status is exactly pending and whose exact due instant is at or before the inclusive caller-owned cutoff.

Matching schedules preserve exact-ID scan order. This intentionally differs from complete =list_due=, whose global result remains ordered by due instant then exact ID. When validated lookahead proves more inventory exists, =next_after= is the last *inspected* schedule ID even when no schedule matched. Otherwise it is absent. The cursor need not identify an existing schedule. Empty stores and cursors beyond the end return empty terminal pages.

The operation works through read-only storage, reads no ambient clock, mutates nothing, persists no cursor, and grants no dense-fill scan, lifecycle, worker, lease, dispatch, retry, permission, or execution authority.

** Alternatives considered
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: alternatives-considered
:END:
*** Slice =ScheduleStore::list_due= in memory
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: slice-schedulestorelist_due-in-memory
:END:
Rejected because output would be bounded while storage selection, replay, and allocation would remain global.

*** Fill a dense page of due schedules
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: fill-a-dense-page-of-due-schedules
:END:
Rejected because future, cancelled, claimed, or materialized inventory can make the work needed to find one match unbounded.

*** Keyset-page by due instant and exact ID
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: keyset-page-by-due-instant-and-exact-id
:END:
Rejected for this slice because due instants are arbitrary =u64= values inside canonical JSON event payloads. SQL JSON numeric ordering cannot preserve every =u64= exactly, and excluding malformed payloads before canonical replay would weaken fail-closed behavior. A durable due index needs a separate schema, migration, validation, and repair decision.

*** Filter ordinary inventory pages in callers
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: filter-ordinary-inventory-pages-in-callers
:END:
Rejected because callers could resume from the last emitted match rather than the last inspected schedule, fail to advance through empty windows, or diverge from the kernel's corruption boundary.

** Consequences
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: consequences
:END:
- Authoritative stream selection and the number of projected schedules are bounded by one explicit exact-ID scan window plus lookahead; replay within each selected stream remains proportional to that schedule's complete lifecycle history.
- Empty nonterminal pages advance safely and continuation windows do not overlap.
- Matching schedules are exact-ID ordered rather than globally due ordered; callers needing earliest-due selection retain =list_due=, =claim_next_due=, and =materialize_next_due=.
- Corruption in the inspected window or lookahead fails closed before partial output. Corruption before the cursor, beyond lookahead, and in unrelated stream families remains isolated.
- Complete due discovery and ordinary inventory and status paging remain unchanged.
- No schema, event, write, clock, worker, or execution authority is added.

** Verification
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: verification
:END:
Strict RED→GREEN tests prove sparse pending-due selection, inclusive cutoff behavior, all-nonmatching progress, exact-ID ordering, non-overlapping continuation, nonexistent and beyond-end cursors, terminal and empty read-only behavior, unrelated-stream exclusion, selected and lookahead corruption failures, and corruption isolation outside the bounded window. The complete repository quality gate must remain green.

** Revisit when
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: revisit-when
:END:
Reconsider before adding a CLI adapter, dense due pages, a durable due-instant index, snapshot tokens or durable cursors, destructive deletion, clocks, workers, leases, dispatch, permissions, retries, or execution.
